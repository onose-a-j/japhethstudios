How it works

From brief to launch in about ten weeks.

A focused website can ship in four to six weeks. An app or web app usually takes eight to twelve.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A kickoff call and short research to learn your users, goals and competitors.

    Weeks 1–2
  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes, then full screens and a clickable prototype you can test with real people.

    Weeks 3–6
  3. 03

    Build

    We build it, or hand clean, documented Figma files to your developers.

    Weeks 6–9
  4. 04

    Launch

    Go-live, analytics setup and a handover session with your team.

    Week 10
Questions

Questions clients ask.

How much does a project cost?

Every project gets a fixed quote after a short call. Pick a budget range in the form below and we'll suggest a scope that fits it.

How long does it take?

A website usually takes four to six weeks. A mobile app or web app usually takes eight to twelve.

Do you build it too, or only design?

Both. We can design and build the whole product, or design it in Figma and hand it to your developers.

Can you redesign my existing site or app?

Yes. We start with a short review of what works today, so the redesign keeps what your users already rely on.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the project, your timeline and a rough budget. The form below asks for all three.
